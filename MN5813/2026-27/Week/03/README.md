# Week 03: Assessment briefing
<!-- /gen -->

*This week explains both assessments. Then you write your first [AI Use Record](../../Guides/glossary.md#term-ai-use-record) entry and shortlist your [briefs](../../Guides/glossary.md#term-brief).*


## Overview

This is a transition week. There is no [Quest](../../Guides/glossary.md#term-quest) and no [Debrief](../../Guides/glossary.md#term-debrief). In the lecture, I'll walk you through the group project, the individual report, and how I [interpret](../../Assessment/interpretation.md) the assessment. For example, you will see how the marking rubric is used in an AI-open module.


## Before class

- [ ] Read the [Introduction](./Introduction.ipynb) if you can: The briefing on both assessments and the key changes
- [ ] Bring your laptop or a way to reach your Codespace
- [ ] Make changes to at least **two** existing files from Weeks 1 and 2
- [ ] Talk to your AI [Associate](../../Guides/glossary.md#term-associate) at least **twice** in your Codespace. Overrule its recommendation at least once
- [ ] Make sure you have 'pulled' this week's materials into your repository


## In class

<!-- gen:values.yml#session.line -->
- Session: 45-minute lecture + 105-minute workshop
<!-- /gen -->

### Lecture

**<!-- gen:values.yml#session.lecture-label -->Lecture (45 min within the 60-minute slot)<!-- /gen -->**

- Briefing (30 min): I'll walk you through both assessment <!-- plain-language: everyday brief -->briefs and the [assessment interpretation](../../Assessment/interpretation.md). The briefing covers the rubrics, the evidence, the AI Use Record, and <!-- plain-language: everyday abcd record -->the record you keep of your own work
- Q&A (15 min): I'm sure you will have questions about the assessment. We will cover them after the walk-through

### Workshop

**<!-- gen:values.yml#session.workshop-label -->Workshop (105 min within the 120-minute slot)<!-- /gen -->**

- Your first AI Use Record entry (15 min):
  - Open a recent [SpecStory](../../Guides/glossary.md#term-specstory) transcript from `.specstory/history/`. You should find it in your Codespace
  - Pick one exchange where you overruled your [Associate](../../Guides/glossary.md#term-associate)
  - Write it up as a "Notable rejections and corrections" entry in the [AI Use Record format](../../Assessment/interpretation.md), ready for your [QuestLog](../../Guides/glossary.md#term-questlog)
- Shortlist your [briefs](../../Guides/glossary.md#term-brief) (80 min):
  - Open the [abcd record primer](../../Assessment/abcd-record.md), the [individual briefs](../../Assessment/individual-briefs.md), and [Template.ipynb](../../Assessment/Template.ipynb)
  - Read the key terms of the [abcd record](../../Guides/glossary.md#term-abcd-record) and its challenges
  - Shortlist two or three briefs that interest you
  - Write one sentence per brief on what its stakeholder would do with the answer
- Assessment questions (10 min): Ask what you still want to know, now that you have worked through the assessments in more detail

Working on your own machine instead of a Codespace is optional. The guide on [Working locally](../../Guides/working-locally.md) (for your own time) covers how to install the same tools there.
