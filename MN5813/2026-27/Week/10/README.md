## Overview

Week 10 is the last teaching week of term. The whole session is the **group presentation**.

The presentation is **formative**: I award no marks for it. See also [group project brief](../../Assessment/group-project.md), section "4. Deliverables and logistics".


## This week's learning objectives

By the end of Week 10 you can:

1. Present your team's questions, findings, and the <!-- plain-language: everyday decision -->decisions behind them in 10 minutes
2. Answer questions on a logged <!-- plain-language: everyday decision -->decision in plain business terms: Your choice(s), checks behind it, and the alternative(s) your team assessed but ultimately rejected
3. Read your team's exported record and identify possible gaps in how you've evidenced it
4. Fill each gap with a retrospective [`DECISION:` entry](../../Guides/glossary.md#term-decision-entry), dated the day you write it
