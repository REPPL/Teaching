# Week 02: Python fundamentals
<!-- /gen -->

*Core Python (<!-- plain-language: everyday decision -->decisions, functions, loops, strings, lists, dictionaries, files), learnt alongside the two AI moves that make your AI pair useful.*


## Overview

This week you build your workstation, a GitHub Codespace, and keep it for the rest of the module. The [Introduction](./Introduction.ipynb), section "Your environment this week", says how your environment changes. You also extend the Python from Week 1 into the full beginner toolkit. You meet the module's four AI moves and practise the first two. The week ends with **[Quest](../../Guides/glossary.md#term-quest) 02: The Coffee Cart Ledger**. You get a full week of till records from Beans of Egham, the coffee cart from Quest 01. The founder of Beans of Egham needs numbers she can check against her till roll. You must judge your [Associate](../../Guides/glossary.md#term-associate)'s drafts before they ship.


## This week's learning objectives

By the end of Week 2 you can:

1. Write and run Python that uses `elif` chains, functions (parameters, defaults, `return`), and loops built with `range()` and `while`. Use string methods, list indexing and slicing, and dictionaries, including lists of dictionaries as data records
2. Write a text file from code, read it back, and turn each line into values you can use
3. Name the four AI moves and match each to its [quest](../../Guides/glossary.md#term-quest) stage
4. Apply **[GENERATE-JUDGE](../../Guides/glossary.md#term-generate-judge)**: Prompt an Associate with a schema (the exact shape you want the answer in) and constraints. Then verify its output against a [Definition of Done](../../Guides/glossary.md#term-definition-of-done) (a hand check plus a plausibility check) before you accept it
5. Apply **[PREDICT-RUN](../../Guides/glossary.md#term-predict-run)**: Write a one-line prediction before you run unfamiliar code, and use each surprise to find a gap in your understanding
6. Judge two working AI drafts, defend your choice in writing, and adapt the chosen draft to a constraint the AI was not told about
