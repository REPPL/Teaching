# Week 09: DataViz III: Design and critical reflection
<!-- /gen -->

*We'll be looking through the charts each team produced. Ideally, you print them out (or send them to me and I'll print), then hang your report figures on the wall, for others to critique them.*


## Overview

This week's workshop is the [Gallery](../../Guides/glossary.md#term-gallery): A peer critique of your group's report figures, using the [Four-D crit](../../Guides/glossary.md#term-four-d-crit) protocol.

Each visiting group writes its crit on a paper [feedback sheet](./feedback-sheet.pdf). The outcomes go into the [design rationales](../../Guides/glossary.md#term-design-rationale) you submit with your report.


## This week's learning objectives

By the end of Week 9 you can:

1. Critique a data-visualisation figure using the Four-D protocol, and keep to its ground rules ([DataViz design guide](../../Guides/dataviz-design-guide.md), section "8. The Four-D crit protocol")
2. Give feedback on a figure for each [TRACE](../../Guides/glossary.md#term-trace) letter, as "I like" and "I wish", and name its biggest problem
3. Propose one redesign change as a "What if…?" question, and explain why it would improve the figure most
4. Type each feedback sheet into your team's record as a [`CRIT` entry](../../Guides/glossary.md#term-crit-entry). Log each accepted change as a [`DECISION:` entry](../../Guides/glossary.md#term-decision-entry) with an owner and a date
5. Revise a figure and its design rationale in response to critique, recording what changed and why
6. Export figures at final size, with consistent styling across a whole report

## In class

<!-- gen:values.yml#session.line -->
- Session: 45-minute lecture + 105-minute workshop
<!-- /gen -->

### Lecture

- The Four-D protocol and its ground rules (15 min)
- I walk through the mock crit from the [Demonstration](./Demonstration.ipynb) live (20 min)
- How the crit outcomes feed the <!-- plain-language: everyday commit -->Commit, your [`DECISION:` entries](../../Guides/glossary.md#term-decision-entry), the design rationales, and Week 10's group presentation (10 min)

### Workshop

Your group shows its two figures (ideally hang them on the wall for everyone to see). In each crit, one group presents a figure, and all the other groups critique it. The session closes with the <!-- plain-language: everyday commit -->Commit, in which your group logs the outcomes. The [Introduction](./Introduction.ipynb) has the schedule.


## At home (about 1 to 2 hours, before Week 10)

- [ ] Implement the accepted redesign changes, and act on each "I wish" your group agrees with. Update each figure's design rationale to cite the crit: What changed, why, and who proposed it
- [ ] Check that every crit outcome is a `DECISION:` entry in your team's record with a named owner
- [ ] <!-- plain-language: everyday commit -->Commit the redesigned figures, the updated rationales, and photos of your feedback sheets to your team repository. Export this week's record there too, as the [Stoa workflow guide](../../Guides/stoa-workflow.md#7-weekly-export-and-backup), section "7. Weekly export and backup", explains
