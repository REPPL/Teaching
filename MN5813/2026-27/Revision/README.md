# January revision session: Individual report Q&A
<!-- /gen -->

*You get quick feedback on your figure and also on how you've framed your project. I explain the [AI Use Record](../Guides/glossary.md#term-ai-use-record), and we look back at the module.*


## Overview

The January revision session is the last taught session before the [individual report](../Assessment/individual-report.md) is due. Bring your report as it stands, even if it is half finished.


## This week's learning objectives

By the end of the revision session you can:

1. Present one report figure with its draft [design rationale](../Guides/glossary.md#term-design-rationale) and act on structured feedback
2. Judge your angle against the viability test in [Introduction.ipynb](./Introduction.ipynb)
3. Write an AI Use Record that meets the specification in the [assessment interpretation](../Assessment/interpretation.md)
4. Assemble the complete individual submission
5. Describe what you can now do that you could not do in Week 1


## Before class (about 1–2 hours)

- [ ] Read the [Introduction](./Introduction.ipynb)
- [ ] Pick the figure you are least sure of. Draft its rationale. Section "6. E: Explain" of the [DataViz design guide](../Guides/dataviz-design-guide.md) gives the form
- [ ] Run the thirty-second check on the primary table your [brief](../Guides/glossary.md#term-brief) names
- [ ] Write the one-sentence question your report answers

Before the session, ask questions other students may also have on <!-- gen:values.yml#links.qa_forum|link:the Moodle forum -->[the Moodle forum](https://moodle.royalholloway.ac.uk/mod/hsuforum/view.php?id=1470467)<!-- /gen -->; I'll address during the session.

## In class

The session runs on <!-- gen:values.yml#weeks.revision.when -->Tuesday 19 January 2027, 14:00 to 17:00 (UK time)<!-- /gen -->. Attendance is optional. The [Introduction](./Introduction.ipynb) explains how to join, in person or online.

### Lecture

- How the session runs, in the room and online *(10 min)*
- The AI Use Record walk-through, with a worked example *(20 min)*
- Submission checklist and module retrospective *(15 min)*

### Workshop

- Feedback on your design rationale *(40 min)*: You get a short round of feedback on your figure in a small group. The [Introduction](./Introduction.ipynb) explains how it runs
- Polishing your brief *(30 min)*: I circulate and give a verdict on your angle. Ask for it once your thirty-second check has run, or post in the session channel if you are online
- Open Q&A on your report *(35 min)*


## At home (the report itself, before submission)

Submit by the deadline in section "7. Timeline and support" of the [individual report brief](../Assessment/individual-report.md). Before then:

- [ ] Make the one change from the feedback round. Revise the figure and its rationale to match
- [ ] Act on the verdict on your angle
- [ ] Finish your AI Use Record
- [ ] Work through the submission checklist in [Introduction.ipynb](./Introduction.ipynb)
- [ ] <!-- plain-language: everyday commit -->Commit and push your repository. Then upload your PDF to Moodle
